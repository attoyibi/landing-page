Developer Job Interview Preparation: Common Questions

An interview is a skill that can be trained, regardless of how good you are at writing code. A tidy answer structure and the right preparation often separate two candidates of equal ability.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Prepare four kinds of questions: introductory, behavioural, technical and case study, in proportions that suit the role.
  • Answer behavioural questions with STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result (and the lesson).
  • For technical questions, restate the question, think aloud, start simple, discuss edge cases and ask if you are stuck.
  • Prepare three STAR stories, a short "tell me about yourself" and your own questions for the interviewer.

Handling technical questions

  1. Restate the question in your own words to make sure you understand.
  2. Think out loud. The interviewer judges how you think, not only the final answer.
  3. Start with a simple solution, then improve it.
  4. Discuss edge cases and complexity.
  5. If you are stuck, ask. Asking for a hint is better than silence.

Preparation checklist

Tip

The questions you ask show your quality. Ask how the team works, the biggest challenges, and how success is measured in this role.

FAQ

How do I answer the salary question?
Research the market range, decide on a figure that is reasonable to you and state it confidently while open to discussion.
What if I am nervous?
Practise out loud, with a friend, or record yourself. Breathe, speak slowly and accept that a little nervousness is normal.
Do I need to prepare algorithms?
It depends on the company. The basics of data structures and complexity are often asked. Check the selection stages the company describes.
