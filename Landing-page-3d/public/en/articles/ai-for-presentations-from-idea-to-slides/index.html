AI for Presentations: From Idea to Tidy Slides

A good presentation starts with a story and a message, not slide design. AI helps a lot at the outline and rehearsal stages, while the decision about the main message stays yours.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start with one sentence of core message, then an outline, then one idea per slide, then rehearsal.
  • Write slide titles as full sentences that state the message, and keep text short and large.
  • Ask the AI to play a sceptical audience and ask hard questions, then practise a 30-second answer to each.
  • AI slides are often full of generic points; replace them with real examples and your own data.

Start with the message, not the slides

Rules for good slides

Rehearsing with AI

  • Ask the AI to play a sceptical audience and ask hard questions.
  • Practise a 30-second answer to each question.
  • Ask for feedback on the confusing parts.
  • Time your talk, then cut what is not essential.
Tip

AI often produces slides full of generic points. Replace them with real examples and your own data. That is what makes a presentation memorable.

FAQ

Can AI make a PowerPoint file?
Some tools can generate slides automatically. You still need to tidy the result and check the content.
What is the ideal number of slides?
There is no fixed number. A rough guide is about one slide per one to two minutes of speaking, but message and rhythm matter more.
How do I deal with nerves?
Practise repeatedly, know the material well and prepare a strong opening. Our article on public speaking goes into more detail.
