AI for Excel and Google Sheets: Formulas and Tidy Data

Many people give up on spreadsheets because of complicated formulas. An AI assistant can write and explain formulas quickly, as long as you can describe the data clearly. And as long as you test the result.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Name four things: the data structure, the goal, the application (Excel or Google Sheets) and one example row with its correct result.
  • SUM, IF, SUMIF and VLOOKUP or XLOOKUP cover most early needs.
  • Test an AI formula on 2-3 rows whose answers you know, and on edge cases like blank cells, zeros and text in number columns.
  • Do not upload spreadsheets with customer or salary data to unapproved services; describe the columns or use sample data.

How to check the AI's result

Data privacy

Do not upload spreadsheets containing customer or salary data to services that are not approved. Just describe the column structure, or swap in sample data.

FAQ

Can AI read an Excel file directly?
Many services support file upload and analysis. Mind the privacy, and still check the result.
Why does an AI formula fail in my Excel?
Differences in version, the formula separator (comma or semicolon), or a function that is not available. State the application and version.
Which formulas should I learn first?
SUM, IF, SUMIF, and VLOOKUP or XLOOKUP cover most early needs.
