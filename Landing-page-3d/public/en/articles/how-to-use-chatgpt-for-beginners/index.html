AI for Everyone

How to Use ChatGPT for Beginners: Your First Steps

ChatGPT and similar AI assistants are now used to write, learn and work. Beginners usually hit the same two walls: not knowing what to ask, and trusting the answer too much. This guide deals with both.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start by stating your goal and giving context: who it is for, the level and the limits.
  • Good beginner tasks: explaining concepts, summarising, drafting, brainstorming and quizzing yourself.
  • Read the answer critically, check important facts, and ask for revisions instead of giving up after one answer.
  • Never enter passwords, card numbers, private health data or confidential documents into a public chatbot.

Common beginner mistakes

Privacy

Do not enter passwords, card numbers, private health data or confidential company documents into a public chatbot. Assume that what you type may be stored or processed by others, unless the service's policy says otherwise.

FAQ

Is ChatGPT free?
There is a free service with limits and paid plans with extra features. What is offered changes over time, so check the official site.
Does ChatGPT always know the latest news?
Not always. Its knowledge is limited to its training data unless the search feature is on. Always check sources for recent information.
Can ChatGPT do my schoolwork?
It can help you learn, but handing in its output as your own breaks the rules of many schools. Use it to understand, not to replace your work.
