AI for Everyone

ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude? How to Choose an AI Chatbot

The list of AI chatbots keeps growing and their features change fast. Rather than hunting for the "best", it is more useful to set your own criteria, so your choice does not go stale with the next update.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start from your need (writing, up-to-date answers with sources, or general questions), not from a brand.
  • Five lasting criteria: quality on your task, language fit, privacy settings, price and limits, and supporting features.
  • Test two or three services with your real task before choosing, and start with the free version.
  • Features change every few months, so check the official site, and keep checking answers and protecting privacy with every chatbot.

Start from the need, not the brand

Five criteria that last

Table 1: Five criteria that last
CriterionWhat to ask
Quality for your taskTry your real task on several services and compare the results
Language fitDo style and accuracy suit you? Test with text you know well
Privacy and data settingsIs there an option to stop conversations being used for training?
Price and usage limitsWhat is free, what are the limits, how much is the paid plan?
Supporting featuresDocument upload, images, web search, integration with apps you use
A lasting tip

Each model changes every few months. Do not get stuck on one brand. Just get into the habit of checking answers and protecting privacy, because that applies to all of them.

FAQ

Which is the smartest?
There is no single answer that lasts. Services excel at different tasks, and rankings change with updates. Test with your real tasks.
Do I need a paid subscription?
Not always. The free version is enough for many needs. Subscribe if you regularly need higher usage limits or extra features.
Can I use several chatbots at once?
Yes, and it is often useful: compare answers on important topics as a way to cross-check.
