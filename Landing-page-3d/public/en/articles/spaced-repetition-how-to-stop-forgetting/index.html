Spaced Repetition: How to Study So You Don't Forget Fast

You study all night before an exam, then a week later you have forgotten most of it. That is normal and can be explained. The good news is that there is a way of studying that uses that forgetting: reviewing just as memory starts to fade.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Memory fades fast at first and then slows; each successful recall makes the memory stronger and slower to fade.
  • Spaced repetition reviews at growing intervals, for example day 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30.
  • Turn material into questions or cards, test yourself before seeing the answer, and lengthen the interval after a correct answer.
  • Re-reading feels comfortable but works less well; retrieval practice (recalling without looking) gives far more long-term benefit.

The forgetting curve

Research since the 19th century, pioneered by Hermann Ebbinghaus, shows that new memories fade fast at first and then more slowly. Each time you recall something, that memory gets stronger and fades more slowly. That is the basis of spaced repetition: reviewing at longer and longer intervals.

A sensible review schedule

How to apply it

  1. Turn the material into questions or cards (one idea per card).
  2. Test yourself: answer from memory before seeing the answer.
  3. A correct answer: lengthen the interval. A wrong one: review sooner.
  4. Use a card app that schedules automatically, or a notebook with dates.
  5. Studying a little every day is better than cramming once.
Important

Reviewing by re-reading feels comfortable but is less effective. Recalling without looking (retrieval practice) gives far more benefit for long-term memory.

FAQ

Does it suit every subject?
Very much for facts, vocabulary, formulas and concepts. For skills, combine it with hands-on practice.
How many new cards should I add per day?
Start with few (for example 10-20), because the reviews pile up. Keep it to what you can manage.
Is there an app for it?
Many card apps schedule automatically. Choose one that suits your needs.
