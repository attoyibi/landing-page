AI for Meetings: Summaries, Minutes and Follow-Up

Meetings often eat two hours, and then nobody remembers who is meant to do what. AI can turn meeting notes or a transcript into a summary, decisions and follow-ups in minutes, as long as you give clear instructions and check the result.

By Muhammad Muchson Attoyibi·October 23, 2026·1 min read

Key takeaways

  • The flow: record or note with permission, get a transcript, ask for a structured summary, then check and send.
  • Ask for a fixed structure: summary, decisions, follow-up list with owner and deadline, and open questions.
  • Tell the AI to write "not stated" when something is unclear and not to add information that is not in the notes.
  • Recording a meeting needs participants' consent and must follow your organisation's policy and the law.

Problems that often come up

Recording ethics

Recording a meeting needs the participants' consent and must follow your organisation's policy and the law that applies. Tell participants the meeting is being recorded and will be summarised by a tool, especially when sensitive matters are discussed.

FAQ

Can AI replace a note-taker?
It can make a quick draft. A human is still needed to check accuracy and keep the nuance.
What if the meeting uses mixed languages?
Many models handle mixed languages well, but names and technical terms need checking.
Is it safe to upload a meeting transcript?
It depends on the service's policy and how sensitive the content is. For confidential meetings, use a service your organisation has approved.
